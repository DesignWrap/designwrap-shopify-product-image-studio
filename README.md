# DesignWrap Shopify Product Image Studio

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

**DesignWrap Shopify Product Image Studio** is a Codex skill for turning uploaded product photos into consistent, catalogue-ready Shopify product images.

It creates a clean, commercially neutral presentation for each suitable source image:

- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) seamless background
- Subtle, natural contact shadow below the product
- Square 1:1 canvas at 2048 × 2048 px by default
- Crisp, high-resolution PNG output without watermarks, borders, frames, or colour casts

### What it protects

The skill changes the background and presentation only. It preserves the product's exact silhouette, colour, materials, texture, seams, logos, labels, hardware, printed text, and proportions. Colour variants and distinct product angles remain separate outputs.

### Best for

- Shopify product-gallery images
- Product-photo cleanup
- Consistent catalogue image batches
- Preparing products for a uniform storefront grid

### Not intended for

- Lifestyle or campaign imagery
- Redesigning, retouching, or inventing product details
- Automatically removing people, pets, or complex scenes where that would change the product presentation

For products photographed on a person, pet, or in a complex scene, the skill asks for direction before removing the subject or scene.

### Default output

One finished PNG is delivered per suitable source image. Products are centred, upright, uncropped, and scaled consistently, typically occupying about 70–85% of the canvas height. Original files are never overwritten.

## 中文

**DesignWrap Shopify Product Image Studio** 是一个 Codex skill，用于将上传的产品照片处理成风格统一、可直接用于 Shopify 商品详情页的专业产品图。

它会为每张适合处理的原图建立干净、中性的电商目录视觉：

- 纯白无缝背景（`#FFFFFF`）
- 产品下方轻微、自然的接触阴影
- 默认 `2048 × 2048 px` 的 1:1 方形画布
- 清晰的高分辨率 PNG，不含水印、边框、相框或偏色

### 严格保留产品细节

本 skill 只调整背景与呈现方式，不会改动产品本身。它会保留原产品的准确轮廓、颜色、材质、纹理、车线、Logo、标签、五金、印刷文字与比例。不同颜色和不同角度会分别输出，不会合并为同一张图。

### 适用场景

- Shopify 商品图集
- 产品白底图清理
- 批量统一产品目录图片
- 为店铺商品网格建立一致的视觉风格

### 不适用场景

- 生活方式或营销活动视觉图
- 重新设计、修饰或虚构产品细节
- 未经确认就移除人物、宠物或复杂场景主体的图片

如果产品拍摄于人物、宠物或复杂场景中，且移除主体可能改变产品展示方式，skill 会先询问处理方向。

### 默认交付规格

每张符合条件的原图输出一张完成图。产品会保持居中、直立、完整不裁切，并在整批图片中维持一致的视觉比例；通常约占画布高度的 70–85%。原始文件绝不会被覆盖。
